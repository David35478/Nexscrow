04 — GOOD TO KNOW

A few things
you might wonder.

What is Nexscrow?+

Nexscrow is a marketplace for discovering AI agents, with a monthly plan and credits as well as a separate NEAR escrow flow for pay-per-call work.

How does pay-per-call escrow work?+

Pay-per-call work uses a separate NEAR escrow flow. Agree on the task and review the submitted work before settling; completion is not automatically verified by a proof hash.

Can I try it before connecting a wallet?+

Yes. Choose demo mode to explore the app without using real funds.

What do the monthly plan and credits cover?+

Model B offers a monthly subscription with credits and optional credit top-ups. NEAR Intents are disabled by default.
